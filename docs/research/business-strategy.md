# RUNG — Business strategy (Milestone 1 draft)

_Draft for Minahil to review. Numbers marked **(assumption)** are planning placeholders, not market facts; replace them with real vendor quotes before launch._

## Positioning

**RUNG (رنگ) — Har Khushi, Har Rang.** Lahore's one place for every celebration: order cakes, flowers, gifts and outfits, and book decor, catering, beauty, photography, venues and entertainment, all from one account, organised by occasion.

- **Against Shadiyana:** we are every occasion, every week, not one wedding a lifetime. Products and services together.
- **Against foodpanda / Daraz:** we are built for gifting and events: scheduled slots, sender/recipient, message cards, custom briefs, bookings with advances.
- **Against Instagram vendors:** we are their storefront and calendar, and the buyer's safety net.

## Business model

| Revenue line | How it works | Starting point |
|---|---|---|
| Commission on product orders | % of item subtotal, deducted at payout | 10–15% **(assumption, test with vendors)** |
| Commission on service bookings | % of booking value, taken from advance | 8–12% **(assumption)** |
| Delivery margin | Only if RUNG arranges courier; otherwise vendor keeps delivery fee | Phase 2 |
| Featured listings / occasion banners | Vendors pay to appear on Eid, wedding-season pages | After 100+ vendors |
| Vendor subscription (optional) | Lower commission for a monthly fee | Phase 2 |

No escrow (per brief): customer payments go to RUNG's merchant account, and RUNG pays vendors out on a schedule after delivery/event completion. Legal and tax set-up for this flow needs a professional's review **before taking real money** (see accounts doc).

## Launch plan (Lahore)

**Phase 0 — Vendor seeding (4–6 weeks before launch).** Hand-recruit ~40–60 vendors in 6 categories: cakes, flowers, gift hampers, decor, makeup/mehndi, photography. Focus on DHA, Gulberg, Johar Town, Model Town, Bahria Town, Cantt. Free onboarding, photo help, first-month zero commission.

**Phase 1 — Soft launch (web + WhatsApp).** Occasion pages: Birthday, Anniversary, Eid, Mehndi/Dholki, Aqeeqa. COD + JazzCash/Easypaisa + bank transfer. Measure order completion, on-time delivery, refund rate.

**Phase 2 — Mobile app + payments + overseas senders.** Card checkout, delivery photo proof, Urdu voice assistant beta.

**Phase 3 — Wedding season push (Oct–Apr).** Packages that bundle decor + catering + photography; vendor calendars; advance/remaining payments.

## Go-to-market

- **Instagram/TikTok** with Urdu-first, colourful creative (templates in `brand/social/`). Weekly "occasion of the week" content.
- **WhatsApp** for order updates and re-ordering ("Ammi's birthday is next week, want to send flowers again?").
- **Vendor referrals:** each vendor shares their RUNG storefront link; their customers become RUNG customers.
- **Overseas Pakistanis:** targeted ads in UAE, KSA, UK around Eid and Mother's Day.
- **Corporate:** Eid hamper catalogue for Lahore offices.

## Metrics to track from day one

Orders per week, booking requests per week, vendor acceptance rate and time, on-time delivery %, cancellation and refund %, repeat customers at 60 days, share of prepaid vs COD, average order value (PKR), active vendors per category.

## Key risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Low trust → COD only, high returns | Verified vendor badge, real-photo reviews, delivery photo, clear refund policy, COD limits for new customers |
| Vendor no-shows on bookings | Vendor acceptance step, advance payment, penalty/ranking rules, backup vendor suggestions |
| Thin margins on cheap items | Minimum order value per vendor, delivery charges set by vendor, focus on gift bundles |
| Chicken-and-egg supply/demand | Seed vendors first, launch with fewer categories done well |
| Payment/merchant onboarding delays | Ship with COD + manual bank transfer; integrate gateways behind a provider-agnostic interface |
| Brand name clash | Run trademark search with IPO-Pakistan before spending on print **(needs Minahil's go-ahead)** |
