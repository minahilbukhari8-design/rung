# RUNG — Lahore market and competitor research

_Milestone 1 · researched 14 Nov 2026 · every figure below links to its source. Anything marked **(verify)** is a lead, not a fact, and must be checked before it is used in a pitch or the product._

## 1. Headline findings

1. **The market is big but cash-first.** Pakistan's e-commerce sector was valued at **US$7.7bn in 2024** (retail e-commerce US$5.4bn), forecast to grow ~17% a year to 2027 ([Profit / PCMI](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2025/03/29/pakistans-e-commerce-sector-hits-7-7bn-in-2024-smes-face-challenges-in-tapping-global-market-report/)). Industry reports put **~95% of online orders on cash-on-delivery**, and only ~24% of the population has a bank account ([US Trade.gov country guide, Mar 2026](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/pakistan-ecommerce)).
2. **Mobile-first is not optional.** PTA counted **198 million 3G/4G users in 2024**, and ~45m Facebook users ([Trade.gov](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/pakistan-ecommerce)). Discovery happens on Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp; checkout happens on phones.
3. **Trust is the main barrier.** Shoppers prefer COD because of "high-profile cases of undelivered products" ([Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2025/03/29/pakistans-e-commerce-sector-hits-7-7bn-in-2024-smes-face-challenges-in-tapping-global-market-report/)). RUNG has to sell reliability (verified vendors, real photos, delivery-slot promises kept, easy refunds) before it sells variety.
4. **Celebrations are a huge, fragmented spend.** The wedding industry alone is estimated at **Rs900bn**, still "fragmented and offline" ([CW Pakistan on Shadiyana's pre-seed](https://cwpakistan.com/shadiyana-raises-800k-pre-seed-funding-to-digitize-pakistans-wedding-industry/)). Birthdays, Eid, aqeeqa, dholki, anniversaries, graduations and corporate events add a high-frequency layer on top that no single platform owns.
5. **Seasonality is predictable.** Peak traffic: Eid-ul-Fitr, Eid-ul-Adha, Black Friday, New Year, and **wedding season (October to April)** ([Trade.gov](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/pakistan-ecommerce)). Add Valentine's, Mother's Day, Independence Day and Ramadan iftar/gifting.
6. **Quick-commerce has thin margins.** Q-commerce players report "thin margins, high delivery costs" and are focusing on density in Karachi and Lahore ([The News, Jul 2026](https://www.thenews.pk/print/1425409-krave-mart-sees-room-for-growth-in-pakistan-s-fragmented-q-commerce-sector)). RUNG should not run its own riders at launch: vendors deliver, or RUNG books a courier per order.

**Takeaway:** nobody in Lahore combines (a) scheduled product gifting and (b) bookable event services under one account, organised by occasion. That combined "occasion" layer is RUNG's wedge.

## 3. Who RUNG serves

**Customers**
- **The organiser (25–45, Lahore, often women):** plans birthdays, dholkis, aqeeqas, Eid gatherings. Wants one place to book decor + order a cake + book a mehndi artist, with prices up front.
- **The remote sender (overseas Pakistanis, other cities):** sends cakes/flowers to family in Lahore. Pays by card, needs sender/recipient split, delivery photo and tracking. Most likely early adopter of online payment.
- **The young gifter (18–28):** last-minute birthday/anniversary surprises, budget-sensitive, Roman Urdu speaker, Instagram native.
- **Corporate buyer:** Eid hampers, office celebrations, bulk orders with invoices.

**Vendors**
- Bakeries and home bakers, florists, gift/hamper makers, boutique fashion; decorators, caterers, makeup/mehndi artists, photographers/videographers, venues, entertainers (dhol, DJs, magicians).
- Their pain: chasing payments, double bookings, no-shows, haggling over WhatsApp, no repeat-customer tools.

## 4. Product implications for later milestones

- **Payments:** COD (products) + JazzCash / Easypaisa wallets + cards + bank transfer with proof upload. Services need **advance + remaining** payments; no escrow (per brief), so cancellation policy and refund rules must be explicit per vendor.
- **Language:** Urdu (Nastaliq, RTL), English, and **Roman Urdu search** ("cake chahiye kal shaam tak"). Search must match "mehndi/mehendi/mehandi".
- **Delivery:** slots (e.g. 2-hour windows), same-day cut-off per vendor, delivery charge by Lahore zone (DHA, Gulberg, Johar Town, Model Town, Bahria, Cantt…). Vendors set zones and charges; RUNG never invents them.
- **Trust features:** CNIC-verified vendors, real-photo reviews, "delivered" photo proof, clear refund flow, WhatsApp order updates.
- **Occasions as first-class data:** Birthday, Mehndi, Dholki, Nikah, Walima, Baraat, Aqeeqa, Eid, Anniversary, Graduation, Baby shower, Corporate, Get well soon, Condolence (muted palette).

## 5. Open questions to validate with real users (before Milestone 4)

1. What share of Lahore buyers will prepay for a cake vs insist on COD? (Survey 30–50 target users.)
2. Will decorators accept a platform commission, or prefer a monthly listing fee?
3. Which 3 occasions drive the most repeat orders? Hypothesis: birthdays, Eid, anniversaries.
4. Overseas sender demand: is card checkout enough, or do they need PayPal-style options?
